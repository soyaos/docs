## [0.1.0-alpha.0] — 2026-05-19

### Added

- Initial Docusaurus 3 scaffold for **docs.soyaos.ai**.
  - `quickstart` — 5-minute walk-through ending in a running `soya:echo`.
  - `architecture` — Planet / Moon / Comet overview.
  - `editions` — deployment comparison across the six editions.
  - `soyapack-v0` — SoyaPack v0 manifest field reference.
  - `cli-v0` — CLI v0 verb reference.
- Sidebar (`sidebars.ts`) grouping the five seed pages under "Getting
  started" and "Reference".
- Static `static/img/logo.svg` placeholder.
